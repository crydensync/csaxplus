Open suggestions
Raise lockout threshold from 3 to 5

8% of lockouts this month resolved with a correct password on the next try — mostly typos, not attacks. A higher threshold would cut accidental lockouts without materially helping a real attacker.audit_events · 30d scan

Shorten idle session timeout to 14 days

Current idle timeout is 30 days. Median time between sessions for active users is 3 days — a 14-day timeout would still cover nearly everyone while shrinking the window a stolen session cookie stays valid.sessions · aggregate

Back off retries for the failing legacy-billing webhook

api.legacy-billing.io has failed its last 6 deliveries with a 503. Immediate retry is hammering an already-down endpoint — switching this endpoint to exponential backoff (starting at 5 min) would reduce load without losing events, since they're queued either way.webhook_deliveries · 6 rows
